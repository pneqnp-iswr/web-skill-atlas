# Quality scoring

Scores are editorial metadata, not popularity rankings. The rubric totals 100 points:

- usefulness — 25
- specificity — 15
- reproducibility — 15
- source quality — 15
- maintenance/freshness — 10
- uniqueness — 10
- documentation — 10

Interpretation: 50 is usable, 70 is good, 85 is excellent, and 95+ should be exceptional. Verification status is separate from score.
